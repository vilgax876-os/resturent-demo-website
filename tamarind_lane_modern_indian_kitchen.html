OUR PHILOSOPHY

Purity of Origin, Reimagined with Grace

"Indian cooking is not mere culinary alchemy; it is our ancestral devotion to patience, firewood, and the medicinal harmony of dry spices."

Founded by Chef Ranveer Oberoi, Tamarind Lane was born out of trips along Kerala’s pepper coast and Lucknow’s royal corridors. We refuse shortcuts: we pound our garam masala in stone mortals every morning, age our basmati for 24 months, and churn our ghee in-house from Gir cow milk.

OUR FOUR FOUNDATIONS

Crafted Without Compromise

Single-Origin Spices

Direct trade from family cooperatives in Wayanad, Guntur, and Kashmir. Sun-dried and unadulterated.

Regional Heritage

Recipes unearthed from royal khansamas and coastal grandmothers across Malabar, Awadh, and Punjab.

Living Charcoal Fire

We strictly avoid gas ovens. All grilling is done over natural mangrove and coconut shell embers for gentle smokiness.

Mindful Hospitality

Rooted in 'Atithi Devo Bhava'. Intuitive dining with tailored allergen consideration and bespoke attention.
